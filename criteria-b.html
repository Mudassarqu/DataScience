How to use each exemplar

  1. Read the Design Brief and compare it with your own community need.
  2. Check the Data Science Link to see what your sensor measures.
  3. Study B1 and write your own measurable success criteria.
  4. Compare the three B2 ideas and invent a fourth.
  5. Check B3, then use the B4 drawings, BOM and worksheet as a model for your plans. Download an STL to open in TinkerCAD.
