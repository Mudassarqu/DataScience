🛠️ Criterion C: Creating the solution

Plan, build, log and justify your changes. Choose the lesson that matches your Criterion B design.

How a Criterion C lesson works

1 · Plan and rubricC1 to C4 with a worked example. Replace every cell with your own plan, photos and data.
2 · Lab 1: virtual ESP32Run your sensor code, test your Criterion B specification, save the readings.
3 · Lab 2: Python data labParse, clean, analyse, decide and report on a full day of readings.
4 · Practice, Learn, QuizShort Python challenges, a video lesson and a quiz. Scores go to your teacher.
5 · Summative bookletDownload the Word booklet, complete it with your own work and submit it.

Lessons by unit

Every card has the five learning pages and the Word summative booklet for that lesson.
